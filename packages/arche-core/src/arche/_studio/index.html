      A decision id is a handle. Paste one and the ledger gives back what was decided, the fields that carried it and the ones that argued against it, and whether the engine installed now makes the same decision.

      Decision id

      Three questions, one id. decision is what was recorded. explain is the evidence sorted into supporting, refuting and missing. replay runs it again from the stored inputs and says whether the same id comes out.

      Values are masked until you ask. The decision is the object here; the identifiers it was made from are not.

      A replay that does not reproduce is not an error. It names what moved: a factor, a pin, an outcome. That is the difference between "something changed" and knowing what.

      Two records, any fields. The engine returns a decision, the evidence behind it, and its reason for holding back when it does.

      Five roles. Origin, destination, via, location, and unknown.

      The cue word decides. From marks an origin, via a waypoint, in a location. Where cues conflict the role is left unknown rather than guessed.

      Watch it get one wrong. In the first example, deliver to the central warehouse in Kano comes back as a location. The nearer in beats deliver to. The rules are a cue lexicon, not a parser.
