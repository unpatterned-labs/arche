# Guides

One job per page. Each opens with what you will have at the end, and every example on it was run against the installed package before it was published.

- [Find and mask personal data](find-and-mask.md): the spans with the statute section attached, and the copy the statute permits.
- [Compare two records](compare-two-records.md): the verdict, its two fields, what every number in the evidence means, and why an identical pair can be `review`.
- [Resolve a batch](resolve-a-batch.md): two lists in, matches and a review queue out, and which scorer ran.
- [Resolve documents](resolve-documents.md): files in, one record per document, resolved against each other.
- [Resolve a delivery address](resolve-a-delivery-address.md): a sentence to a verified endpoint, one question, or a refusal, against a list you own.
- [Extract to your schema](extract-to-your-schema.md): your field names as the labels, one declaration for extraction and matching.
- [Keep, explain, replay](keep-and-replay.md): the ledger, and what it knows once a few decisions are in it.
- [Review a queue](review-a-queue.md): the report, the studio, applying outcomes, and evaluating against a truth set.
- [Let an agent call arche](mcp-server.md): the MCP server, its tools, and three ways to see it work.
- [Serve over HTTP](serve-over-http.md): the same verbs behind a port, with the receipts.
