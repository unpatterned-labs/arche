Five roles. Origin, destination, via, location, and unknown.

The cue word decides. From marks an origin, via a waypoint, in a location. Where cues conflict the role is left unknown rather than guessed.

Watch it get one wrong. In the first example, deliver to the central warehouse in Kano comes back as a location. The nearer in beats deliver to. The rules are a cue lexicon, not a parser.
